# Dimensional Recursion Hypothesis

**Status:** hypothesis-level extension of Perfect Recursion / BER  
**Not established as a physical law**

This document explores whether the structural logic of Perfect Recursion can be applied to dimensional identity, representation, and possible dimensional emergence.

The extension deliberately distinguishes a **formal structural claim** from a **physical historical claim**.

The formal claim is that a higher-order structure can contain lower-order structure without becoming identical to the earlier lower-order state.

The physical historical claim — for example that reality literally evolved through a sequence such as `0D -> 1D -> 2D -> 3D` — remains unverified and is not asserted by the current theory.

## 1. Core distinction: structure versus expression

A state can display a lower-dimensional pattern without the containing structure becoming lower-dimensional.

For example, inside a two-coordinate structure `(x, y)`:

```text
(1, 1)
(1, 0)
```

both are states of the same two-dimensional coordinate structure. The second lies on the subset `y = 0`, but the ambient structural identity remains two-dimensional.

Therefore:

> **lower-dimensional expression != lower-dimensional structural identity**

The same distinction applies more generally to projections, constrained motion, rendered images, files, simulations, and other representations.

## 2. Higher-order containment does not erase lower structure

The geometric analogy is:

```text
point -> line -> surface -> volume
```

The intended recursive reading is not that each later stage destroys the earlier one.

A line still contains point-relations. A surface still contains line-relations. A volume still contains surface-relations.

More generally:

```text
lower-order structure
        ↓ same-level relation / organization
new higher-order relation
        ↓
higher-order structure containing the lower-order structure
```

This mirrors the Perfect Recursion principle that higher-level formation does not require lower-level disappearance.

## 3. Same-level interaction can generate higher-order structure

The hypothesis proposes a generative pattern:

> **units at one structural level can interact, combine, or organize so that a new relation becomes available at a higher structural level.**

A schematic form is:

```text
D_n elements / relations
        ↓ interaction
new relation not represented as an isolated D_n element
        ↓
D_(n+1)-like structural organization
```

This is a structural analogy, not yet a demonstrated physical law of spatial dimension generation.

Examples such as points composing lines, lines composing surfaces, pixels composing images, characters composing sentences, and frames composing video illustrate cross-level information construction. They do not by themselves prove that physical spacetime historically emerged through dimensional stages.

## 4. Projection and representation are not downgrade

A higher-dimensional structure can generate a lower-dimensional representation without returning to a lower-dimensional historical state.

Examples:

```text
3D model -> 2D render
4D relation -> 3D/2D projection
3D scene -> 2D image file
```

These operations preserve the higher-order system while producing a lower-dimensional artifact, projection, or encoding.

Therefore:

> **representation is generation, not structural reversal.**

If the higher-order state remains active, the operation belongs to the current recursion.

## 5. Dimensional identity changes with structural context

A lower-order structure embedded in a higher-order structure does not have the same historical identity as an earlier state in which that lower order was the complete accessible structure.

Conceptually:

```text
historical D_n state:      D_n

later higher state:        D_(n+1)
                           └─ contains D_n-like substructure
```

The contained `D_n`-like structure may be mathematically equivalent in some local relations, but it is not historically identical to the earlier complete `D_n` state because its context and relational identity have changed.

Thus:

> **containment != restoration**

and:

> **structural equivalence != historical identity**

## 6. Access to higher dimensions is observation-limited

Assume a hypothetical higher structure:

```text
(x, y, z, i)
```

and an observer whose accessible state is only:

```text
(x, y, z)
```

Represent observation by:

```text
P(x, y, z, i) = (x, y, z)
```

If two higher states differ only in `i`:

```text
(x, y, z, i1)
(x, y, z, i2)
```

then they are observationally indistinguishable to that observer unless variation in `i` changes something inside the accessible coordinates or otherwise modifies the observation map.

This does not prove that an extra coordinate `i` exists. It states the access condition under which an unrepresented degree of freedom would be directly unobservable.

The observer may still infer a missing variable if effects inside the accessible structure cannot be adequately explained without it.

This links dimensional recursion to the existing Perfect Recursion principle that **perception itself is recursively revisable**.

## 7. Constancy is still part of the changing whole

A local variable can remain numerically constant while the containing system continues to evolve.

```text
A(t1) = A(t2)
```

does not imply that `A` has remained in the historical state of `t1`. Its relational context at `t2` belongs to the later system state.

Therefore a higher-order structure cannot be divided into one part that literally remains in an earlier historical stage while another part continues history merely because the first part's local value is unchanged.

> **local constancy is a state relation inside ongoing change, not exemption from the system's history.**

## 8. True structural downgrade and true reversal

The extension adopts the state-return criterion from `PROVENANCE_RECONSTRUCTION.md`.

Suppose a system has genuinely moved from a lower-order structural state `D_n` to a higher-order structural state `D_(n+1)`.

Then producing a lower-dimensional representation while retaining the higher-order state is not a return:

```text
D_(n+1)
   ├─ remains active
   └─ generates D_n'
```

This is branch/artifact generation inside the current recursion.

A **true structural downgrade**, under the current definition, would instead require the active higher-order state itself to be returned such that the additional higher-order relations are no longer retained as part of the active system:

```text
D_n -> D_(n+1)
then, hypothetically:
D_(n+1) -> D_n
```

If the system still contains information or relations that exist only because it reached `D_(n+1)`, then the result is not identical to the earlier `D_n` state.

Therefore:

> **apparent dimensional reduction while the higher-order state remains is not true downgrade.**

## 9. Relationship to recursive branching

If a higher-order structure creates a new lower-order artifact or simulation while remaining active, the new object is part of ongoing recursion:

```text
D_(n+1) continues
└─ generates D_n'
      └─ D_n' may continue its own recursion
```

This is another instance of the general BER rule:

> **recursion can generate recursion.**

A second lower-dimensional history therefore does not show that the original higher-dimensional state was reversed.

## 10. Relationship to provenance discontinuity

If higher-order structures can become autonomous and their historical generators can disappear, then the earliest dimensional state accessible to a later observer need not be the absolute first dimensional state.

Conceptually:

```text
D_0 -> D_1 -> D_2 -> D_3

later accessible provenance:
? -> D_2 -> D_3
```

This does not establish that such dimensional history actually occurred. It only shows that, under Perfect Recursion, **absence of recoverable lower-dimensional provenance would not logically prove absence of an earlier generating stage**.

## 11. What this hypothesis does not claim

The current dimensional extension does **not** claim that:

- physical reality has been experimentally shown to evolve `0D -> 1D -> 2D -> 3D`;
- points literally existed as a physical universe before lines, surfaces, or volumes;
- mathematical dimension and every form of informational hierarchy are identical concepts;
- conventional uses of “effective dimensional reduction” in physics are invalid terminology;
- a fourth spatial dimension or coordinate `i` has been observed;
- true global dimensional reversal is physically possible.

These require independent physical and mathematical evidence.

## 12. Hypothesis-level principles

### DR-H1 — Higher-order containment
A higher-order structure MAY preserve lower-order structures or relations without becoming identical to their earlier historical state.

### DR-H2 — Expression is not identity
A state expressed on a lower-dimensional subset MUST NOT be classified as a structural downgrade solely because some coordinates are fixed or unused.

### DR-H3 — Projection is not return
A projection, representation, simulation, or generated lower-dimensional artifact SHOULD be treated as a present operation of the containing structure, not as restoration of an earlier dimensional history.

### DR-H4 — Higher-dimensional access is conditional
A variable outside an observer's accessible representation is directly distinguishable only if it changes accessible observations or the observer's representational capacity changes.

### DR-H5 — Higher-order formation can retain lower-order structure
Formation of a higher-order structure need not erase the lower-order relations from which it is composed.

### DR-H6 — True downgrade inherits the state-return criterion
If a claimed downgrade retains higher-order relations or internal information that exists only because the higher-order state occurred, it is not identical to the earlier lower-order state.

### DR-H7 — Apparent downgrade can be branch generation
If the higher-order state remains while a lower-order object is generated, the operation belongs to ongoing recursion and may create a new recursive branch.

### DR-H8 — Historical dimensional emergence remains unverified
The sequence `D_n -> D_(n+1)` as a literal history of physical spacetime remains a hypothesis and MUST NOT be promoted to a core Perfect Recursion invariant without independent evidence.

### DR-H9 — Same-level coupling is not automatic dimensional ascent
An individual and its directly encountered environment MAY coexist and interact within the same recursive dimension. Mutual influence, mutual state change, or treating their relation as an experimental unit MUST NOT by itself be classified as generation of a higher dimension.

## 13. Test and falsification program

The extension separates two kinds of claims.

### A. Structural / definitional tests

A candidate system should distinguish:

- ambient structural dimension from lower-dimensional expression;
- projection from structural identity change;
- generated lower-dimensional artifacts from reversal;
- historical identity from local mathematical equivalence;
- same-level interaction from genuine higher-order emergence.

A formalism that cannot make these distinctions is insufficient for this extension.

### B. Physical historical hypothesis

To support literal dimensional emergence in nature, independent evidence would be needed for at least one of the following:

- a mechanism by which lower-dimensional physical structures generate additional independent degrees of freedom;
- empirical remnants predicted specifically by such a transition;
- a mathematical model yielding current spacetime structure from lower-dimensional predecessor states;
- observations that distinguish this hypothesis from existing dimensional, emergent-spacetime, or effective-field descriptions.

Without such evidence, the dimensional-emergence part remains speculative.

## 14. Same-dimension initialization of the minimal individual model

For the current minimal experimental model, the initial relation is explicitly:

```text
D0:
A <-> E
```

`A` is the initial individual and `E` is its directly encountered environment.

Both are treated as participants in the same initial recursive dimension `D0`.

Therefore:

```text
A != automatically higher-dimensional than E
E != automatically higher-dimensional than A
A + E != automatically D1
```

Their mutual interaction may change both:

```text
A0 <-> E0
↓      ↓
A1 <-> E1
↓      ↓
A2 <-> E2
```

but same-level co-evolution is not by itself dimensional ascent.

A `D1`-like structure must be identified by an additional higher-order relation or organization produced through recursion, not by relabeling the existence of interaction as a higher dimension.

## 15. Compact formulation

> **Dimensional Recursion is a hypothesis-level extension of Perfect Recursion in which higher-order structures may contain lower-order structures without restoring their earlier historical identity. Lower-dimensional expression, projection, or simulation inside a higher-order system is not true dimensional return. Same-level interaction between an individual and its environment does not automatically create a higher dimension. If genuine structural downgrade exists, it must return the active higher-order state itself rather than preserve that state while generating a lower-dimensional representation or branch.**
